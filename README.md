## 🌷 About Me

I build **data and AI systems** with an interest in the engineering underneath them.

My current focus sits at the intersection of:

```text
Data Engineering
      ↓
Reliable Data Systems
      ↓
LLM Applications
      ↓
RAG & Retrieval
      ↓
Agentic AI
      ↓
Production AI Systems
```

### Current interests

* 🏗️ **Data Engineering** — ETL/ELT, orchestration, warehouses, batch & streaming pipelines
* ⚙️ **Distributed Data** — Spark, Kafka, streaming systems and scalable processing
* 🧠 **LLM Engineering** — RAG, embeddings, vector search, evaluation and observability
* 🕵️ **Agentic AI** — tool use, MCP, AI agents and multi-step workflows
* 🚀 **Production Engineering** — APIs, Docker, CI/CD, monitoring and deployment
* 🔍 **Data Intelligence** — finding structure, anomalies and hidden patterns in complex datasets

I am particularly interested in the point where **data infrastructure becomes the foundation for intelligent systems**.

## 🧩 Engineering Philosophy

> **Understand the system, not just the syntax.**

I prefer to learn technologies through the chain:

```text
Problem
  ↓
Architecture
  ↓
Abstraction
  ↓
Implementation
  ↓
Failure Modes
  ↓
Testing
  ↓
Deployment
  ↓
Monitoring
```

The goal is not to collect tools.

The goal is to understand **why a system works, where it breaks, and how to make it reliable**.
